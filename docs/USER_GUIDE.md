## Core Features

### 1. Spaced Repetition Learning

The app uses the SM-2 algorithm (SuperMemo 2) to optimize your learning:

- **New Cards**: Fresh concepts you haven't seen before
- **Learning Cards**: Concepts you're currently mastering
- **Review Cards**: Previously learned concepts scheduled for review

**How to use it:**
1. Navigate to "Spaced Repetition" screen
2. Start your daily session
3. Rate each card: Again (0), Hard (1), Good (2), or Easy (3)
4. The app automatically schedules future reviews

**Best practices:**
- Study regularly (daily is ideal)
- Be honest with your ratings
- Focus on understanding, not memorization
- Review explanations carefully

### 2. Quiz Game Mode

Test your knowledge with multiple-choice questions:

- **Categories**: Ranges, Push/Fold, Exploits, PLO, MTT
- **Difficulty Levels**: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert
- **Instant Feedback**: Get explanations after each answer

**Tips for Success:**
- Read questions carefully
- Use the process of elimination
- Review explanations even when correct
- Track which categories you struggle with

### 3. Range Trainer

Master preflop ranges for 6-max cash games:

- **Positions**: UTG, MP, CO, BTN, SB, BB
- **RFI Ranges**: Learn optimal raising ranges
- **3-Bet Ranges**: Understand polarized vs linear strategies

**How to practice:**
1. Select a position
2. Review the range chart
3. Test yourself with random hands
4. Check if hand is in the correct range

### 4. Push/Fold Charts

Essential for tournament play:

- **Stack Depths**: 10bb, 15bb, 20bb
- **All Positions**: UTG through Button
- **Nash Equilibrium**: GTO-based shove ranges

**Study Method:**
1. Start with Button (widest range)
2. Move to earlier positions (tighter ranges)
3. Practice until you can recall ranges without looking
4. Use in real tournaments!

### 5. Exploitative Guide

Learn to adjust against common player types:

- **Leak Recognition**: Identify opponent tendencies
- **Exploitation Strategies**: Adjust your play profitably
- **Expected Win Rates**: Understand the value of each exploit

**Key Exploits:**
- **Limpers**: ISO-raise aggressively
- **Calling Stations**: Stop bluffing, bet bigger for value
- **Fit-or-Fold**: C-bet 100% of hands
- **Over-3-Bettors**: Call down lighter, trap more

### 6. PLO Training

Specialized training for Pot Limit Omaha:

- **Hand Selection**: Learn strong PLO starting hands
- **Equity Understanding**: PLO equity runs closer
- **Draw Recognition**: Wrap draws, flush draws, blockers
- **Postflop Play**: Navigate complex multi-way pots

1. **Start with the 7-Day Training Plan**
2. **Focus on one category at a time**
3. **Review explanations thoroughly**
4. **Don't worry about speed initially**
5. **Build a consistent study habit**

### For Intermediate Players

1. **Use Spaced Repetition daily**
2. **Focus on weak categories**
3. **Practice Speed Drills regularly**
4. **Upload and analyze hand histories**
5. **Study exploitative adjustments**

### For Advanced Players

1. **Master all categories at Hard/Expert**
2. **Maintain 90%+ accuracy**
3. **Use analytics to identify leaks**
4. **Contribute to community discussions**
5. **Apply learnings at the tables**

**Daily (15-30 minutes):**
- 10 minutes: Spaced Repetition reviews
- 10 minutes: New content or quizzes
- 5 minutes: Review analytics

**Weekly:**
- Complete 7-Day Training Plan
- Focus session on weakest category

## Analytics & Tracking

### Performance Dashboard

Track your progress with:
- **Daily XP Chart**: Visualize your study consistency
- **Accuracy Trends**: See improvement over time
- **Category Performance**: Identify strong and weak areas
- **Study Time**: Track total time invested

### Interpreting Your Data

**Accuracy by Category:**
- 90%+: Mastered
- 75-89%: Proficient
- 60-74%: Learning
- <60%: Needs focus

**Learning Insights:**
- Green trends: Improving (keep going!)
- Flat trends: Plateau (try new study method)
- Red trends: Declining (reduce difficulty)

## Hand History Analysis

### Uploading Hands

1. **Export from Poker Site**:
   - PokerStars: Tools → Instant Hand History
   - GGPoker: My Game → Hand History → Export
   - 888poker: My Account → Hand History

2. **Upload to App**:
   - Select poker room format
   - Choose .txt file
   - Wait for processing

3. **Review Analysis**:
   - GTO recommendations
   - Exploitative adjustments
   - Mistake identification

### Understanding Analysis

**GTO Analysis:**
- Compares your play to solver recommendations
- Shows EV loss for each decision
- Suggests alternative lines

**Exploitative Analysis:**
- Identifies opponent tendencies
- Recommends adjustments
- Estimates win rate improvements

## FAQ

**Q: How long should I study daily?**
A: 15-30 minutes is optimal. Consistency matters more than duration.

**Q: What if I break my streak?**
A: Don't worry! Streaks are motivational tools. Just start a new one.

**Q: Why did my accuracy drop?**
A: The app uses adaptive difficulty - it gets harder as you improve!

**Q: How do I unlock new content?**
A: Most content is unlocked from the start. Some advanced modules require completing prerequisites.

**Q: Can I reset my progress?**
A: Yes, in Settings → Advanced → Reset Progress (cannot be undone).

**Q: How accurate are the hand history analyses?**
A: Analyses are based on modern solvers and GTO principles, but poker is complex - use as a guide, not gospel.

**Q: What's the difference between Spaced Repetition and Quizzes?**
A: Spaced Repetition optimizes long-term retention using scientific scheduling. Quizzes test current knowledge.

**Q: Can I suggest new questions?**
A: Yes! Contact us through Settings → Feedback.
